These are Latin-subset WOFF2 files:
- fredoka-latin-600.woff2 and nunito-latin-800.woff2 were made on October 28, 2026
  with fontTools (pyftsubset) from the brand kit's static instances Fredoka-600.ttf
  (version 2.001) and Nunito-800.ttf (version 3.602), keeping the kern and liga
  features and these characters: U+0000-00FF, U+0131, U+0152-0153, U+02BB-02BC,
  U+02C6, U+02DA, U+02DC, U+2000-206F, U+2074, U+20AC, U+2122, U+2191, U+2193,
  U+2212, U+2215, U+FEFF, U+FFFD (this includes × U+00D7).
- nunito-latin-400.woff2 and nunito-latin-700.woff2 come from the Fontsource
  package @fontsource/nunito, version 5.3.0.

The website loads them from this folder. It does not request Google Fonts
or any other font host.
